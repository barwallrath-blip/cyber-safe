Stay Safe Online

Simple cybersecurity awareness for everyday users.

What is Phishing?

Phishing is when attackers try to trick people into revealing passwords or personal information by pretending to be trusted sources.

Strong Passwords

A strong password includes uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, symbols, and should be at least 12 characters long.

Two-Factor Authentication

2FA adds an extra layer of security by requiring something you know (password) and something you have (phone or token).

Security Tips

  • Never click suspicious email links
  • Enable 2FA on all accounts
  • Use a password manager
  • Keep your software updated
